## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can create challenges for organizations, including increased recruitment costs, productivity loss, knowledge loss, and workforce instability.

However, identifying the patterns behind employee turnover can be difficult when HR data is stored in raw spreadsheet format.

This dashboard was developed to help answer questions such as:

- Which departments have higher employee attrition?
- How does attrition vary across salary levels?
- Is there a noticeable relationship between overtime and attrition?
- How does job satisfaction relate to employee attrition?
- Which job roles have higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary across different experience groups?
- Which employee segments may require greater retention attention?

---

## 🔍 Business Problems Addressed

The dashboard provides an interactive way to investigate several potential attrition-related factors:

### 🏢 Department-wise Attrition
Compare employee attrition across different departments to identify areas that may require further investigation.

### 💰 Salary & Attrition
Analyze attrition patterns across different salary slabs to explore whether compensation levels may be associated with employee turnover.

### ⏰ Overtime & Attrition
Compare attrition between employees who work overtime and those who do not to identify potential workload-related patterns.

### 😊 Job Satisfaction & Attrition
Explore attrition across different satisfaction levels to identify possible employee engagement concerns.

### 👨‍💼 Job Role-wise Attrition
Identify roles with relatively higher attrition and potential retention challenges.

### 📅 Experience & Tenure Analysis
Analyze attrition patterns across different employee experience and tenure groups.

## 💡 Detected Patterns & Suggested Actions

Beyond visualization, this project also focuses on interpreting observed patterns and considering potential HR actions.

### ⏰ Overtime & Attrition

**Observed Pattern:**  
Employees working overtime show a different attrition pattern compared with employees who do not work overtime.

**Suggested Action:**  
Review workload distribution, overtime practices, staffing levels, and work-life balance initiatives.

---

### 💰 Salary & Attrition

**Observed Pattern:**  
Attrition varies across different salary slabs.

**Suggested Action:**  
Consider compensation benchmarking, salary reviews, and role-based compensation analysis where appropriate.

---

### 👨‍💼 Job Role & Attrition

**Observed Pattern:**  
Certain job roles show comparatively higher attrition.

**Suggested Action:**  
Develop role-specific retention strategies, career development opportunities, and employee feedback programs.

---

### 😊 Job Satisfaction & Attrition

**Observed Pattern:**  
Attrition patterns vary across different job satisfaction levels.

**Suggested Action:**  
Strengthen employee engagement initiatives, feedback mechanisms, manager support, and workplace improvement programs.

---

### 📅 Experience / Tenure & Attrition

**Observed Pattern:**  
Employee attrition varies across different experience and tenure groups.

**Suggested Action:**  
Introduce targeted mentoring, career development, onboarding, and retention initiatives for higher-risk employee groups.
